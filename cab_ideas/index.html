Exploring the EA Assistant

Three ideas to try during the session — from automated assessments to filling data gaps and tailoring the Assistant to your enterprise context.

SAP LeanIX Customer Advisory Board · Berlin · 13 October 2026

Three ways to explore

Each idea is self-contained — pick whichever matches your interest. Prompts, setup, and screenshots included.

1

Perform an automated Application Assessment

Use the EA Assistant to run an automated Application Assessment against rules you define — Security, Architecture Review Board, or any criteria that fit the Assistant's context.

Setup
  • Your workspace contains an Application called MaMa CRM.
  • Its Architecture Documentation is attached as a Resource: mama-crm-arc42.pdf.
  • Workspace Files already include an exemplary Application Review — Top 20 Criteria.pdf.
Execution
  • All the context above is accessible to the EA Assistant.
  • Trigger the assessment with a prompt such as:
Evaluate MaMa CRM against the Top 20 assessment criteria
  • Tweak the prompt — e.g. request an evidence register or confidence scores.
Supporting visuals
2

Fill Data Quality Gaps

Let the EA Assistant automatically fill data quality gaps — by reading files in context or searching the web. It proposes values and lets you edit in bulk.

Setup
  • The metamodel has an additional field on the Provider fact sheet: EU_HEADQUARTERED.
Execution
  • The EA Assistant reads and understands your metamodel out of the box.
  • Prompt it to fill the new fields, e.g.:
Research EU_HEADQUARTERED for all my Providers and propose the results in the Inventory Bulk Update
Supporting visuals
3

Tailor the EA Assistant to your company

Tailor the Assistant to your specific company needs — through Architecture Decisions or Workspace Files. The Assistant will reason on these and follow them in prompts.

Setup
  • No specific setup prepared — bring your own context.
Ideas to try
  • Use the EA Assistant to answer frequently asked questions.
  • Use the EA Assistant to answer in the context of your strategic and company objectives.
